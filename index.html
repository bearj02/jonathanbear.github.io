Project

Here are a few past projects I've worked on.

3D Map of Northeastern Tunnels

  • Constructed 3D Tunnel Model: Utilized RTABMAP, ROS2, CUDA, and ZEDm camera to build 3D pointcloud model of Northeastern’s tunnel system. Generated model provided information necessary to calculate total paintable surface area of tunnels and determine amount of paint required to completely cover tunnel walls.

Adaptive Cruise Control System

  • Designed Adaptive Cruise Control Model: Built Simulink model to simulate autonomous steering logic utilizing STM32K144 board and Haptic Wheel inputs to mimic driver behavior
  • Incorporated Logic for Multiple Vehicles: Implemented critical distance as safety measure to avoid collisions with other simulated vehicles. Vehicle information communicated over CAN Network, requiring CAN 2.0 logic to send and receive accurate information

Adaptive Action Selection Algorithm

  • Implemented Adaptive Action Selection Algorithm: Designed testing environment for Adaptive Action Selection algorithm described in “Active Inference and Behavior Trees for Reactive Action Planning and Execution in Robotics” (C. Pezzato et al.) to determine algorithm efficacy compared to its components
  • Utilized Open-Source Libraries: Py_trees, pymdp libraries incorporated to mimic Behavior Trees, Active Inference selection algorithms. Building off existing open-source libraries allowed for more customizable scene creation, leading to more accurate and replicable metrics

P100-AM Modbus

  • Improved Product Modularity: Designed automated modular I/O mapping Arduino codebase for ProductivityOpen P1AM-100 Modbus allowing client flexibility to rearrange attached modules, reducing in-person installation requirements

Autonomous Tennis Ball Collector

  • Designed Autonomous Tennis Ball Collector: Integrated Intel RealSense camera with Jetson Nano on a mobile robot. Modeled and fabricated fitted attachment in SolidWorks to secure collected tennis balls, and implemented SLAM and A* path planning with April Tags to collect tennis balls while avoiding court lines

JamaCloud Data Collection Requirements Database

  • Optimized Data Management: Transitioned data collection requirements from Google Sheets to JamaCloud prototype workspace enhancing organizational structure, facilitating efficient revision tracking, and significantly improving data integrity and accessibility for the team
  • Advanced Automation and Integration: Leveraged REST API capabilities to synchronize updates across platforms and perform complex calculations not inherently supported by JamaCloud. This automation reduced manual effort, increased accuracy, and streamlined workflows, demonstrating a tangible impact on operational efficiency
  • Leadership and Communication: Acted as subject matter expert on JamaCloud, presenting results and strategic advantages to team members. Fielding questions and addressing concerns fostered a deeper understanding and appreciation of JamaCloud’s benefits, which in turn facilitated its integration into daily operations and decision-making processes
